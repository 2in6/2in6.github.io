Hi, I’m 2in6 👋

I’ve been building Minecraft servers and plugins for around five years.

Most of what I make starts as a passion project. I want something to exist, nobody’s built it the way I’d want it, so I write it myself. Some of that ends up public. A lot of it just runs on servers I’ve worked on.

Work

Fighting Bots

Plugin, in progress

Adds bots to your server that actually fight. They pick targets, aim, time their hits and react to knockback, so fighting one feels closer to fighting a player than fighting a mob.

Almost everything about how they fight is configurable.

Not out yet. It’ll go up on Modrinth when it’s finished.

Spyglass

Plugin

Lets staff open a player’s inventory and enderchest. It does that and nothing else, which was the point.

Death Kick

Plugin

Removes a player from the game when they die, for hardcore and scripted servers. Kept small on purpose: no config bloat, nothing it doesn’t need.

Servers

I build full servers, and I mean the whole thing: setup, configuration, plugins, permissions, hosting. Sometimes I’ll bring in a builder, but most of the time it’s just me.

The ones I’ve run sat somewhere around 5 to 50 active players. I haven’t started a new one in a while, mostly because doing it properly costs money I’d rather not burn. The one I actually want to make is a modern PvP server with duels and FFA, done right.
